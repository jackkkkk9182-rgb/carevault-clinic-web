Privacy Policy

CareVault processes claim documents and patient details on behalf of your hospital, which remains the data fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We act as a processor.

Each hospital's data is isolated under its own CV-ID and stored encrypted. We use it only to deliver audits, packets and ledgers, and we do not sell it or use it for advertising.
